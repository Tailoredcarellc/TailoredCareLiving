Housing & support

A home first.
Support when needed.

Tailored Care Living provides the residential home. Residents who need help with daily living may arrange services through qualified outside referral partners, based on individual needs and eligibility.

A clear division of care

  1. We provide the home
  2. Residents maintain their routines
  3. Partners assess support needs
  4. Eligible services come to the home

Tailored Care Living does not directly provide nursing, assisted-living, medication administration, or personal-care services. Those services, when appropriate, are delivered separately by qualified outside providers.

About the home & eligibility

What is Tailored Care Living?

Tailored Care Living provides affordable furnished shared housing for qualified adults seeking a clean, safe place to live and move forward.

Is this assisted living?

No. Tailored Care Living provides residential housing and does not directly provide nursing care, medication administration, or personal-care services. Qualified outside referral partners may provide separate support to eligible residents.

What if a resident needs daily-living assistance?

Residents may work with qualified outside referral partners for additional support based on individual needs, provider requirements, and eligibility. Partner services are separate from the monthly housing arrangement.

Are meals provided?

No. Residents are responsible for purchasing and preparing their own food.

Who may apply?

Returning citizens, veterans, older adults, and other qualified adults facing housing barriers are welcome to apply. Every application is reviewed individually.

Application & private details

Where is the home located?

For resident privacy and security, property information is provided to appropriate applicants during the application process.

How much does it cost?

The housing rate is $800 per month per bed. Utilities, internet, and television are included. Availability, room arrangements, and final placement details are confirmed during the application process.

Will I have my own room?

Room arrangements and current availability are discussed during the application process.

What happens after I join the list?

Tailored Care Living reviews your information and may contact you as opening plans and availability develop. Joining the list does not guarantee acceptance, placement, timing, or availability.

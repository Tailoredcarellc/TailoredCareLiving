Our commitment

Accessibility Statement

Tailored Care Living LLC is committed to providing a website that is welcoming and usable for as many people as possible, including people with disabilities.

Our approach

The website uses semantic structure, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, readable type, strong color contrast, descriptive alternatives for meaningful images, accessible form labels, and reduced-motion support.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing practice. As content and services evolve, we intend to review the experience and address barriers that are identified.

Need assistance?

If a website feature prevents you from accessing information or submitting an application, please use an available business contact method once published. Contact details are intentionally hidden until verified rather than displaying inaccurate information.

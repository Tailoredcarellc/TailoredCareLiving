Website policy

Website Terms

Informational purpose

This website provides general information about Tailored Care Living LLC and its shared-housing model. Information may change and is not a promise of housing, availability, pricing, services, or eligibility.

Housing and outside services

Tailored Care Living provides residential housing and is not assisted living, nursing care, medical treatment, rehabilitation, or a personal-care service. Any additional daily-living support is provided separately by qualified outside providers, subject to their own assessment, eligibility requirements, availability, agreements, and fees.

Applications

Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance, placement, availability, or a response within a particular timeframe. Housing arrangements are discussed individually with appropriate applicants.

Representative imagery

Lifestyle and residential imagery represents the intended experience and does not depict the actual Tailored Care Living property.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the website, attempt unauthorized access, submit false information, or interfere with its operation.

Tailored Care Placement Agency

Request a placement.

For individuals, families, case managers, discharge planners, and probation or parole agents. Please do not include detailed medical information; we will discuss needs privately by phone.

Tailored Care Placement Agency helps adults find safe, supportive housing in Milwaukee and the surrounding areas, including people leaving a hospital, treatment program, or incarceration.

What happens next

  1. You send this request. It takes about 10 minutes.
  2. Our team reviews it and calls you privately to talk through needs and options.
  3. We work with you on a placement that fits. Submitting a request does not guarantee placement.

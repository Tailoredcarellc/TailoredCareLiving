Website policy

Privacy Policy

Effective August 17, 2026

Tailored Care Living LLC respects the privacy of housing applicants and website visitors. This policy describes the limited information collected through this website.

Information we collect

When you submit a housing application, we collect the contact, housing, independent-living, income, and optional contextual information you choose to provide. We do not ask for Social Security, bank, payment-card, Medicare, or benefit account numbers.

How information is used

Application information is used only to review housing inquiries, communicate with applicants, and determine whether to continue a placement conversation. Applicants are not subscribed to promotional marketing through this form.

Delivery and retention

Applications are securely transmitted to Tailored Care Living by email. The website does not intentionally maintain a permanent application database. Email service providers necessarily process submissions to deliver them.

Security

We use server-side validation, input sanitization, spam controls, and encrypted HTTPS connections in production. No internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may choose not to submit the application. Please do not include sensitive identifiers or unnecessary medical details in any free-text field.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the website and business practices develop. The effective date will be revised when material changes are published.
